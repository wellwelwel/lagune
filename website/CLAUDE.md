# Lagune website

## Reduced motion

A visitor who sets `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is asking for less motion, not for none. Answer the setting by softening the motion: slow it down, shorten how far it travels, or trade a movement for a fade. Never stop an animation, switch transitions off, make a change instant, or hide an element because of it.

- Why: large, fast, or sudden movement is what unsettles some people, and that is what the setting asks to tone down. Freezing the page instead drops the feedback that tells the visitor what changed.
